Two ways to earn

One machine, two roles.

Run either role, or both on the same computer. Lending uses your CPU and GPU for jobs. The ledger role uses very little.

How much $TasQ do I need to hold?

The amount is published at launch, together with the contract address. It is the same for both roles.

Is there a presale?

No. 100% of the supply goes into the liquidity pool on a fair launch platform. The team buys its 10% on the open market.

Can one machine do both roles?

Yes. Lending uses your CPU and GPU for jobs. The ledger role is light and can run alongside it.

Where do ledger rewards come from?

Three sources: 2.5% of supply emitted over the first 3 months, buybacks funded by the token tax, and network fees. More verified work means more rewards. No fixed rate is promised.

Where do I verify my wallet?

In the TasQ app. Sign in with Privy and the app checks your $TasQ balance on Robinhood Chain.
