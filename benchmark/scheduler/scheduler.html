Scheduler

tasqnetwork.io/benchmark/scheduler

A discrete-event simulation of mode R with hidden audits on 300 nodes across three GPU speed classes. 10% of nodes are adversarial and 5% are flaky with a 15% fault probability. Each run processes 20,000 units with r = 3, q = 2 and audit rate π = 0.1. Seat selection is either uniform at random or weighted by κ (reputation, price and latency).
