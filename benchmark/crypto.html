Reading this

The hybrid handshake and an ML-DSA-65 signature together cost about 10.4 ms, paid once per session, not per token. ML-DSA-65 signing dominates. Verification is about 0.2 ms, which is the side a client pays.

Library versions: cryptography, blake3 1.0.10 and pqcrypto, as pinned in the script. Timings on GPU servers will differ. Run the script on your own machine to compare.
